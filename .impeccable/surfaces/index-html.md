Scope: home page (index.html) plus six project pages under /projects/. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads), with a Persuade duty: a recruiter must see real work and real numbers inside thirty seconds on a phone.

Audience: hiring managers for AI solutions and implementation roles, arriving from a CV link. Action: open a project, click through to the live site or source.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The six builds sit on a darkroom proof sheet: numbered frames of real screenshots, film-edge print carrying label and stack, keepers marked in yellow china marker. Refuses the dark-portfolio card grid with neon accent and glowing borders.

OWN-WORLD: Film-base black #0c0c0b, print off-white #ecebe4, edge-print grey #8a8a84, one china-marker yellow #f2c230 used only as hand marks (boxes, underlines, circles). Archivo at condensed widths for the name and project titles, Archivo normal for body, Martian Mono only for edge-print data (frame numbers, labels, stack). Sprocket-hole band for the tools strip. No gradients, glow, glass, rounded cards.

STORY: Visitor reads the name and one line, sees six frames in sequence, reads the two marked results (93 leads at £4.62, prep 40 to 10 minutes), opens a frame, reads problem/build/stack/result, clicks out to live site or source.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Name in monumental condensed caps across the full width, top-left; one-line role and links (GitHub, LinkedIn, Email) beneath, small. Three-sentence intro. First row of frames begins inside the first viewport on desktop (frame 01 top visible), on phone the intro ends at the fold with frame 01's edge print showing.

FORM: Contact Sheet, my candidate 5 of 7, seed key 0774063a. Signature interaction: hovering or focusing a frame draws a hand-marked china-marker box around it (on touch, frames get boxed once as they cross mid-screen); result figures carry static marker underlines/circles.
