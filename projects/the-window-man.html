The problem

What I built

An instant quote calculator on the site. Domestic and commercial jobs go through the same flow.

The enquiry then moves into WhatsApp, where an AI agent confirms the details before handing over to the owner.

Stack

  • Custom quote calculator

  • WhatsApp AI handover

Result

Pure Water Ready proved the build. This one proved I had a product.

Same system, a different round. I changed the pricing logic and rebuilt nothing else.

This client came in through Facebook ads. That was my first evidence that paid ads were the channel that worked.
