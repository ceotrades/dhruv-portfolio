Plyo

An iOS app that turns workout videos saved on TikTok and Instagram into a training plan.

View source

Built as a portfolio piece. Runs on my own device through Expo. Not on TestFlight, not on the App Store.

The problem

People save workout videos and never do them.

A saved post doesn't tell you what to do on Tuesday.

I built Plyo to prove I can build a product end to end. I'm not planning to launch it.

What I built

You send a saved video into the app. It reads the video, pulls out the exercises, sets and reps, and builds a training calendar around what you've collected.

A body map shows the muscle groups you've worked, so you can see what you're skipping.

How it works

Expo and React Native, with expo-router for navigation. Supabase handles auth, the database and the backend, and the work happens in edge functions:

  • extract-workout retrieves the video with Apify, then has OpenAI read the workout out of it.
  • generate-calendar builds the plan.
  • delete-account deletes the account and all of its data.

Service keys live in environment variables. The only key in the repo is Supabase's public anon key, which is designed to ship in the app.

Result

A working build that runs on my phone.

It covers auth, a database schema, an AI extraction pipeline that has to cope with messy real input, and the parts people skip, like proper account deletion.
