Website, instant quote calculator, CRM and the ads behind it, for a window cleaning round in West Sussex.

View live site

The problem

Window cleaners lose work to whoever answers first.

The old site had a contact form. An enquiry arrived while the owner was up a ladder, and he got to it four hours later. Every enquiry needed him in person before a price existed.

What I built

I replaced the contact form with an instant quote calculator. The customer sees a price before anyone speaks to anyone.

Behind it I set up automations in GoHighLevel:

  • Speed to lead. A new enquiry gets a reply in seconds.
  • Email nurture. Anyone who gets a quote and doesn't book goes into a follow-up sequence.
  • Missed call text back. A call that rings out still holds the enquiry.

The enquiry then moves into WhatsApp. An AI agent confirms the details and hands over to the owner to book.

I run the Meta ads that point at the calculator and manage them every month.
