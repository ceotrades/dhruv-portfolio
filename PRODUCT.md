# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No framework, no build step, no npm dependencies. Deploys to Vercel as a static site with no build command. Pinned by the user's brief.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers for AI solutions, automation and implementation roles. They land from a link on Dhruv's CV, often on a phone, and decide in under thirty seconds whether real work exists. Secondary: anyone Dhruv sends the link to from an application form.

## Product Purpose

Get Dhruv Kapoor (Crawley, UK) hired into AI solutions and implementation roles. The site proves that systems he built shipped and worked: client lead systems with real ad results, a client CRM, a personal trading dashboard, an iOS app, and a three-agent content system.

## Positioning

Evidence of shipped, working systems for real small businesses, with real numbers where they exist. Not an engineering portfolio: implementation roles want proof something went live and did its job.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a CV link or application form. Half open it on a phone. They scan the home page, open one or two project pages, and click through to a live site or GitHub repo.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home page plus one page per project under `/projects/`. Images under `/public/<slug>/`.
- Dark theme, near-black ground, off-white text, one accent colour (pinned by brief).
- Semantic HTML, alt text on every image, visible keyboard focus.
- No cookie banner, analytics or tracking.
- Project pages: title, one-line summary, buttons (`View live site`, `View source` only where source is public; no disabled buttons), large screenshot, then The problem, What I built, Stack, Result. Cut any section with nothing true to say.
- Client domains are live: purewaterreadywindowcleaning.co.uk, thewindowmanpembs.co.uk, noveconcierge.com. GEMINI and Content Agent live/source URLs, GitHub and LinkedIn URLs are placeholders until Dhruv supplies them.

## Brand Commitments

- Voice: blunt, first person, plain, specific. No adverbs, no em dashes, no "passionate" or "cutting-edge", no three-item rhythm lists, short sentences.
- Zyntell is not mentioned anywhere. No ownership or agency language (matches the CV, which titles the role "Operations & Delivery").
- Purewater Ready: do not name the owner or the town. The Window Man: do not name the owner.
- The Content Agent may name and link the TikTok account @ceotalks23.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project content: `Claude Brain/Job Search/portfolio-site-prompt.md` (authoritative), plus READMEs in `Portfolio showcase/{Ai Content agent, Gemini Trading dashboard, plyo}`.
- Purewater Ready results, September 2026: 93 leads at £4.62 on £429.35 spend; 22,879 reached, 571 clicks; CPL £8.40 to £3.23 across the month; campaign life 26 Aug to 30 Sep, £492.10, 101 leads, £4.87.
- GEMINI: morning prep forty minutes to ten.
- Content Agent: 17 outliers, 8 patterns, 25 creator baselines, 18 ranked ideas, 67 posts tracked, 12 learnings, 1,278-view baseline.
- Screenshots on hand: purewater, windowman, nove, content-agent. Missing: gemini, plyo.
- Absent and never to be fabricated: figures for The Window Man and NOVÉ, testimonials, client names beyond the business names, App Store status for Plyo.

## Product Principles

1. Real work visible in under thirty seconds, on a phone.
2. Every number is a real number. Where none exists, say what the project proved instead.
3. The work leads; the site recedes.
4. Honest status labels: client work, live site, personal build, not on the App Store.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on the dark theme, keyboard focus visible, semantic landmarks, alt text on every image, reduced-motion respected.
